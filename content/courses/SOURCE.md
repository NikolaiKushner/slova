# Where the grammar courses come from

The JSON in this folder is ours: short rules in Russian, example sentences
from everyday English, exercises written for Slova. Nothing here is copied
from a textbook.

Two Cambridge books were used as a **syllabus only** — which topics exist, in
what order, and which mistakes Russian speakers make. Their wording, tables
and exercise sentences stay in the books.

| Book | Role |
|---|---|
| *Essential Grammar in Use* (Russian edition), Raymond Murphy with Olga Sands, 2017, ISBN 978-1-316-62996-3 | A1–A2 cut: forms / negatives / questions as separate lessons. |
| *English Grammar in Use*, 5th edition, Raymond Murphy, 2019, ISBN 978-1-108-45765-1 | Later contrast courses (simple vs continuous, perfect vs past) and the idea of a mixed test. Not the first course — the author says it is not for elementary learners. |

The PDFs are not in this repository.

First four courses, in the order we will ship them:

1. Present Simple
2. *to be* (am / is / are)
3. Present Continuous
4. Past Simple
